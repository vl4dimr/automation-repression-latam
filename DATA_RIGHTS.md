# Source attribution and rights

`02_data/SOURCES.md` records the original data providers, versions, URLs and project access-date license notes. Those notes are provenance, not a fresh legal determination or a blanket license for this combined panel. Provider rights and attribution requirements continue to apply to derived columns and outputs.

The project records CC BY-SA 4.0 for V-Dem/ERT, CC BY 4.0 for Penn World Table and World Bank material, and CC0 for SWIID and the Mass Mobilization Project. It records provider-specific/open-data terms for UN Comtrade, ILOSTAT and CEPALSTAT, academic-use terms for Financing the State and Political Terror Scale, and noncommercial-use terms for Freedom House. Review each provider's current terms and the archived vintage before further redistribution; rights that were not established by the source notes are not supplied by this archive.

The anonymous ZIP contains country-level derived analysis data for review and reproducibility. Raw provider archives, the underlying NBER paper, downloaded publisher pages and local software installers are excluded. Citation information remains in the source documentation. The packager does not remove share-alike, attribution, noncommercial or provider-specific conditions, and does not relicense the merged dataset or the research software.

Before any public repository publication, verify that the intended distribution satisfies each upstream condition; use provider links and reconstruction instructions for material that cannot be redistributed. No public deposit, accession, approved journal exception or paid-source permission is represented as already obtained.
